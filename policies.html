Store Policies, Refunds & Privacy

Everything you need to know about digital product delivery, replacement guarantees, and personal data protection.

1. Digital Product Delivery & Refund Policy

Due to the instant, irrevocable nature of digital downloads (immediate access to cloud Google Drive folders containing hundreds or thousands of high-definition video files), we do not offer cash refunds once the unlock password has been issued and verified.

However, your purchase is completely safeguarded by our 100% Lifetime Link Replacement Guarantee:

  • If a Google Drive link ever returns an error, broken permissions, or 404 code, message us on Instagram DM (@giveme_8) with your transaction ID or bundle code.
  • We will provide an active mirror link or alternative cloud download link within 15 to 60 minutes.
  • If we are unable to restore your access within 24 hours, you will receive a replacement bundle of equal or greater value of your choice completely free of charge.

2. Privacy & Data Handling Policy

We take creator privacy seriously. We never sell, rent, or share your contact information with third-party advertisers.

  • Instagram Conversations: Direct messages are strictly used for order fulfillment, link sharing, and technical support.
  • UPI & Banking Data: All payments are processed directly within bank-grade Indian UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm). We do not store or capture your debit card, credit card, or banking PINs.

3. Intellectual Property & Copyright Notice

ReelVault PRO curates digital video assets under commercial distribution agreements, creative commons zero licenses, and royalty-free creator archives. If you represent a rights-holder and believe content has been included without appropriate attribution, contact us at Support@betterbundle.in, and we will promptly review and remove the material within 24 business hours.
