What is a reels bundle?
A reels bundle is a pack of ready-made short videos (often 500 to 10,000+) that creators download and post on Instagram, YouTube Shorts, or Facebook. Bundles are usually faceless and sorted by niche, such as motivational, AI, luxury, or fitness.

They provide an instant foundation for theme pages and agencies, saving hundreds of hours of filming and video rendering.

Can I post bundle reels on Instagram without getting flagged?
Yes, you can post them freely. All reels in our bundles are clean and pre-screened for commercial social media publishing.

Are PLR and MRR reels legal to resell in India?
Yes. Private Label Rights (PLR) and Master Resell Rights (MRR) are legitimate commercial licensing models that give you the right to re-distribute or sell the content to clients and other creators.

However, you cannot claim trademark ownership over unedited raw footage. You are licensed to edit, monetize, and bundle them freely.

Yes. All video assets are standard 1080x1920 MP4 files fully supported by CapCut (mobile and PC), VN Video Editor, Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, and Canva Video.
