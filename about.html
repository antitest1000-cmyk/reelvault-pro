The Story of Get Bundle

Why We Built India's #1 Curated Reels Marketplace

We believe that creating high-earning short-form video content shouldn't require ₹50,000 in camera gear or 40 hours of rendering every single week.

The Problem with Modern Short-Form Creation

In early 2025, our team noticed a frustrating reality: thousands of talented young creators in India wanted to start theme pages on Instagram and YouTube Shorts, but they hit an immediate wall. Western stock footage libraries charged $49/month (over ₹4,000 INR), required international credit cards, and provided horizontal 16:9 clips that looked terrible when cropped to vertical 9:16.

Meanwhile, popular free stock sites had the exact same 10 clips that every other creator was using. It was impossible to stand out or build genuine audience retention.

Our Solution: High-Bitrate, Curated Vertical Asset Vaults

We launched Get Bundle with a simple promise: curated, high-retention 9:16 vertical video assets in viral niches (AI avatars, luxury supercars, gym discipline, kinetic animations), delivered instantly through a private Google Drive link for the price of a cup of coffee (₹99 to ₹199).

There are zero recurring subscription traps. You buy once, unlock via a verified server-side password, and keep lifetime access to download and re-download anytime.
